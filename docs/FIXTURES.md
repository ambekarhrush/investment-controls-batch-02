# Fixture provenance

All inputs in `projects/*/data/fixtures` are deterministic and synthetic. They are generated by `investment_controls.fixtures` and are committed so reviewers can inspect each calculation without credentials or network access.

Dates, vendor names, holdings, transactions and results do not describe a live portfolio or real provider. Historical scenario labels indicate replay-shaped shocks; they are demonstrations and are not claims about exact historical calibration or forecast performance.
